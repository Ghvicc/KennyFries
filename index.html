The place
It wasn't always called Kwa Kenna.

The food brought people in. The people gave it a name.

Kenny's started simply: fries, chicken, smokies, samosas and a place to sit down and eat. Then something happened. People started returning. Friends started meeting there. Students made it part of their routine. Gym guys had their orders. Couples found their corner. Families brought the kids. Somewhere along the way, Kenny's stopped being just Kenny's. It became **Kwa Kenna.**
The Kenny's philosophy

Come for the food.
Stay for the vibe.

A good meal feeds you. A good place makes you feel like you belong. Kenny's has always tried to be both.
